## Full description — max 4000

```
Counting points at the end of a hand is the boring part of the game, and almost everyone gets the primiera wrong. This app does it for you.

Free and ad-free. Counting at the table needs no account and works offline.

POINTS IN EVERY HAND

• Cards — to whoever takes more than 20; tied at 20, nobody scores
• Coins — to whoever takes more than 5; tied at 5, nobody scores
• Settebello — to whoever takes the seven of coins
• Primiera — to the highest total; tied, nobody scores
• Sweeps — one point per sweep

THE PRIMIERA, DONE PROPERLY

The primiera adds up the best card in each suit, and the values do not follow the order of the cards: the seven is worth 21, the six 18, the ace 16, and face cards the least. A team with no card in a suit scores zero for that suit and loses the point without noticing.

Assign it by hand if you already know it, or pick the best card in each suit and let the app do the arithmetic.

YOUR HOUSE RULES

• Games to 11 or 21 points, reached or exceeded
• Napola: ace, two and three of coins score three, plus one per extra consecutive coin
• Queen of coins
• Primiera can be switched off, for tables that do not count it
• Level at the target score? A tie-breaking hand is played

BUILT FOR THE TABLE

• Fast entry at the end of a hand, no pointless screens
• Catches impossible counts: if the cards do not add up to 40, it tells you
• History of every hand with a breakdown of the points
• Entered something wrong? Edit or delete a saved hand
• The match is stored: close the app and find it where you left it
• Light and dark themes

LEAGUES, IF YOU WANT THEM

Create a league, invite people with a code and play together: whoever starts a match is the only one who runs it, everyone else watches it update live. That needs an account, and you sign in with a code sent by email — no passwords.

PRIVACY

Without an account nothing leaves your phone. With one we process only what leagues need: email address, display name and matches, on servers in the European Union. No advertising, no tracking, no profiling, no sharing with third parties.

OPEN SOURCE

The source code is public on GitHub under the MIT licence: anyone can read it, verify it and suggest improvements.

```

### A note on the terminology

The Italian names are kept untranslated on purpose — *settebello*, *primiera*,
*napola* have no English equivalents, and anyone searching in English
for this game already knows them. "Coins" for *denari* and "sweep" for *scopa*
are the accepted translations in English-language descriptions of the game.
